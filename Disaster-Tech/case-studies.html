CASE STUDIES

Technology Helping During Disasters

🌀

Cyclone Monitoring

Satellites and weather monitoring systems help track the location and movement of cyclones.

This information supports early warnings and evacuation planning.

🌊

Flood Monitoring

Sensors, satellites and GIS can help monitor rainfall, water levels and areas that may be affected by flooding.

Authorities can use this information to support emergency planning.

🌍

Earthquake Response

Seismic sensors can detect earthquake activity and provide information about the location and intensity of an event.

Drones and mapping technologies can also support post-disaster damage assessment.

🔥

Forest Fire Monitoring

Satellite imagery and remote sensing can help identify fire activity over large areas.

This information can support firefighting and evacuation decisions.

🚁

Drone-Based Rescue

Drones can provide aerial views of areas that may be dangerous or difficult for rescue teams to reach.

They can help teams understand the situation before entering an affected area.

🗺️

GIS for Risk Mapping

GIS can combine geographic information with disaster data to identify vulnerable locations.

This can help planners develop evacuation routes and emergency response plans.

KEY LESSON

Why Technology Matters

01

Early Information

Technology can provide important information about hazards and changing conditions.

02

Faster Communication

Communication systems can help authorities share warnings and emergency information quickly.

03

Better Decisions

Maps, sensors and data can help emergency teams understand conditions and plan their actions.

04

Safer Response

Tools such as drones and remote sensing can provide information while reducing unnecessary risks to response teams.
