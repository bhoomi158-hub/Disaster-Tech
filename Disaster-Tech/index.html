INTRODUCTION

Technology in Disaster Management

Technology plays an important role in disaster management. Modern technologies such as satellites, drones, GIS, artificial intelligence and early warning systems help authorities monitor hazards, communicate warnings and coordinate rescue operations.

KEY TECHNOLOGIES

Tools That Help Save Lives

🛰️

Satellite Technology

Satellites help monitor cyclones, floods, wildfires and other disasters.

🚁

Drones

Drones can survey disaster affected areas and assist rescue teams.

🗺️

GIS

GIS helps identify vulnerable areas and plan evacuation routes.

🤖

Artificial Intelligence

AI can analyze large amounts of data to support disaster prediction and response.
