DISASTER SAFETY

Stay Safe. Stay Prepared.

Learn basic safety measures that can help people prepare for and respond to common disasters.

SAFETY FIRST

Disaster Preparedness

Disaster preparedness means taking steps before an emergency occurs. A basic emergency plan, essential supplies, communication methods and knowledge of evacuation procedures can help communities respond more effectively.

01 — EARTHQUAKE

Earthquake Safety

🏠

Drop, Cover and Hold

Drop to the ground, take cover under a sturdy object if possible, and hold on until the shaking stops.

🚪

Stay Away From Hazards

Stay away from windows, glass, heavy objects and anything that could fall.

🔌

Check for Hazards

After the shaking stops, be alert for damaged buildings, fires, gas leaks and electrical hazards.

02 — FLOOD

Flood Safety

🌊

Move to Higher Ground

If flooding is occurring or an evacuation is ordered, move to a safer higher location.

🚫

Avoid Floodwater

Do not walk or drive through moving floodwater because its depth and current may be difficult to judge.

📻

Follow Official Information

Monitor official emergency information and follow evacuation instructions from authorities.

03 — CYCLONE

Cyclone Safety

🌀

Follow Warnings

Pay attention to official weather forecasts and cyclone warnings.

🏠

Stay Indoors

Stay inside a safe building and keep away from windows during dangerous conditions.

🎒

Emergency Kit

Keep essential items such as water, food, medicines, a torch and communication devices ready.

04 — FIRE

Fire Safety

🔥

Raise the Alarm

Alert people nearby and contact the appropriate emergency services when a fire occurs.

🚪

Use Safe Exits

Leave through a safe exit and never use an unsafe route because it may expose you to smoke or fire.

🚫

Do Not Re-enter

Once outside, stay in a safe location and do not return to the building until authorities say it is safe.

BE PREPARED

Basic Emergency Kit

01

Water

Keep safe drinking water available.

02

Food

Keep suitable non-perishable food.

03

First Aid

Keep basic first-aid supplies ready.

04

Torch & Radio

Keep communication and lighting equipment available.
