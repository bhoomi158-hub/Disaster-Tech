Role of Technology in Disaster Management

Learn how modern technologies help predict disasters, issue warnings, coordinate rescue operations and support recovery.

MODERN TECHNOLOGY

Technologies Used in Disaster Management

🛰️

Satellite Technology

Satellites provide images and information about weather systems, cyclones, floods, forest fires and other large-scale disasters.

🗺️

Geographic Information System

GIS helps authorities map disaster prone areas, identify vulnerable populations and plan evacuation routes.

🚁

Drones

Drones can provide aerial images, inspect damaged buildings and help rescue teams search difficult areas.

🤖

Artificial Intelligence

AI can analyze large amounts of data and support disaster prediction, risk assessment and emergency response.

📡

Internet of Things

IoT sensors can monitor water levels, temperature, structural conditions and other warning indicators.

📍

GPS

GPS technology helps emergency teams locate people, identify positions and navigate affected areas.

⚠️

Early Warning Systems

Early warning systems provide alerts about hazards such as cyclones, floods, tsunamis and severe weather.

📱

Communication Technology

Mobile networks, radio and emergency communication systems help authorities share important information during disasters.

TECHNOLOGY'S ROLE

Before, During and After a Disaster

01

Before Disaster

Technology helps monitor hazards, assess risks, prepare evacuation plans and issue early warnings.

02

During Disaster

Technology supports emergency communication, rescue operations, navigation and situation monitoring.

03

After Disaster

Technology helps assess damage, coordinate relief activities and support recovery planning.

04

Future Technology

AI, robotics, advanced sensors and improved communication systems can strengthen future disaster management.
